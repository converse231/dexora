## The core gameplay loop

### Takeaway
Each session runs: explore a zone, catch residents (stealth or weaken-then-throw), fill the area's catch-completion %, and spend region currency at the region's Branch. That unlocks weather control and rare-form spawns. Between sessions you hatch eggs and run the farm at home.

### Cited Findings
- The map shows which Aniimo spawn in each zone and their weather and quest conditions. Zones carry level ranges, and Breezy Plains scales to Lv 50. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- Leyline Crystal Clusters are fast-travel points. They show the local Aniimo, your collection status for each, and their spawn conditions and times. — [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)
- Each region has a Branch. Collecting Lumin powers the region up, which unlocks manual weather control (this changes spawns) and weekly Sparkling egg encounters. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- Area Badge progress gates rare-form spawns:
  - The Bronze badge needs 40% Area Catch Completion.
  - With Bronze, you submit 30 Lumin Amber at the Branch to unlock "Nurture".
  - Silver unlocks "Ecosystem Intervention", which spends Vein energy to change an area's weather.
  - Free Nurture: one energy infusion daily at 04:00, up to 3 banked; extra runs cost "Primegy".
  - Source (unverified fan data): [AniimoVerse](https://www.aniimoverse.com/guides/prismana)
- Lumin collectibles sit in chests guarded by aggressive Aniimo, in hidden areas, and behind platforming or racing challenges. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- The Aniimopedia tracks the collection and unlocks rewards, and some creatures open new routes. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
- A beta player reached Lv 35 in about 10 hours. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- Long-tail research: catching 1,000 of a species (31 eligible species) pays a Sparkling Sigil. — [aniimo.kr (unverified)](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)

### Inferences
- The distinctive loop is "catch % per area → regional badge → rare-spawn levers (weather, Nurture)". It turns completion into the key that opens rare hunting, which is directly portable to a per-map completion gate.

### Gaps
- No source describes daily quest or commission structure in detail beyond the 04:00 reset.

## How catching works

### Takeaway
Catching happens in real time. You aim and throw an Aniipod and see a displayed capture percentage. Odds rise with stealth (sleeping, facing away, from behind), with BREAK (a stagger gauge) or low HP, with level difference, and with pod tier. Alpha bosses give one throw after defeat. Rarity is layered: a Sparkling colour (1 in 20,000 wild), Prismana and other Forms, Umbral, and a Potential rating.

### Cited Findings
- **Basic throw:**
  - You enter catch mode, aim and throw an Aniipod, and a capture % is shown.
  - The % depends on the target's state (asleep, fleeing, facing away) and the pod's quality.
  - Source: [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- **What raises the chance:**
  - Official guidance: approach discreetly, catch while it sleeps, or deal enough "rupture" damage. Chance depends on state, HP, level difference, pod type and attacking from behind. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
  - Observing a target first helps, and sleeping Aniimo at night greatly increase the chance. — [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)
- **BREAK:**
  - Attacks drain a yellow BREAK gauge. At zero the target can't act and can be caught with "Quick Catch". — [aniimotools.dev](https://aniimotools.dev/help/combat/)
  - Captures are far more reliable against a Broken target. Researcher Level unlocks capture-enhancing skills. — [HostedGG](https://hostedgg.com/blog/aniimo-beginners-guide)
- **Companion "Catch" behaviour:** your Aniimo fights only when you attack or are attacked, and stops when the wild target's HP is low. — [aniimotools.dev](https://aniimotools.dev/help/combat/)
- **Pod tiers:** Aniipod, Pro, Mega, Ultra, and Sparkling Cube.
  - Ultra guarantees the catch with Elite+ potential.
  - The Sparkling Cube guarantees the catch, Perfect potential and a Sparkling colour. It costs 3,000,000 Credits, or 1,000 Sparkling Crystals at an outpost vendor.
  - Only Ultra and the Cube affect egg hatching.
  - Source (unverified fan data): [aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained); [AniimoVerse](https://www.aniimoverse.com/guides/prismana); [Wikily](https://wikily.gg/aniimo/sparkling-and-prismana)
- **Alpha (boss) catching:**
  - Alphas are giant field enemies that drop a chest.
  - After defeat you get ONE catch attempt; on a miss you must defeat it again.
  - Example: Glynsera at 7.2% with a basic Aniipod and 100% with Ultra or the Cube.
  - Caught Alphas have normal stats but stay bigger.
  - 14 areas each have an Alpha Egg that hatches that area's Alpha.
  - Source (unverified): [aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)
- **Named bosses:** 14 Alphas (Tubster, Leafy, Scorchhowl, Stellarys, Geoclaw, Ignitis, Blazen, Minespine, Irisal, Turbo, Luminelle, Magmarex, Bouldus, Grizbo) and 2 Omegas (Sherro, Tuckin). Both enter "BREAK Recovery" and take extra damage while broken. — [aniimotools.dev](https://aniimotools.dev/help/combat/)
- **Sparkling (shiny equivalent):**
  - Wild rate is 1 in 20,000 (0.005%) for most species.
  - Ten styles (Types I–X, 9.9% each), plus Dazzling (1% wild) and Shadow (only from Egg Heist eggs, 1%). Looks only, no stat change.
  - A "Windchaser Badge" gives +50% Sparkling and Umbral spawn rate in Breezy Plains.
  - 10 Sparkling Sigils convert an owned Aniimo to Sparkling. Sigils come from reporting a Sparkling at an outpost, from 1,000-catch research, or from a vendor at 1,000,000 Credits per day.
  - Source (unverified): [aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)
- **Prismana (rare form):**
  - The highest-grade Form, with base stats equal to the Basic form. It usually gains or swaps an element. There are 25 of them.
  - It spawns only during "Prismana Flow" weather: one per region at a time, non-repeating.
  - Ways to trigger Flow:
    - Nurture: 3% base chance per Nurture, with a pity counter.
    - Prismana Crystal: 3 per day.
    - Natural weather.
  - A Nurture spawn is announced with a beam of light over the spawn point.
  - Source (unverified): [AniimoVerse](https://www.aniimoverse.com/guides/prismana); [aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)
- **Umbral:** a dark, "Vein-charged" variant from world spawns and Egg Heist eggs. It is always Perfect potential, and the look is lost on evolution or form change after a confirm prompt. — [aniimo.kr (unverified)](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained); [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
- **Forms:** Basic, Region, Thunderstorm, Rainstorm, Nighttime, Snowfield, Prismana and more; the Form Gallery lists 220 environment and regional forms. Same base stats, but the element, look or evolution branch can change. One form per Aniimo. — [aniimo.kr (unverified)](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)
- **Stacking:** Prismana can combine with Sparkling, Perfect or Umbral but not with another Form. Alpha + Sparkling + Perfect is possible. — [aniimo.kr (unverified)](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)
- **Spawn conditions:** spawns depend on biome, weather, special phenomena and time of day. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
  - There are four time-of-day bands, and a rare phenomenon ("Cheonhwi" in Korean) brings special Aniimo. — [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)
- **Community reports:**
  - Your companion Aniimo can sometimes attack and kill a rare target before you throw. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
  - Some Aniimo are obtained by trading items. — [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)

### Inferences
- **Weather-gated forms (220 of them):** a creature looks different and gains an element under rain, storm, night or snow. This is a cheap way to multiply collectibles without new species.
- **One throw after a boss win:** a clean "boss catch" mechanic.
- **Displayed catch %:** the game shows the catch percentage before the throw (Dexora's rail already prints `liveMult`).

### Gaps
- The exact catch formula was not found.
- The Umbral wild rate is not published.
- No legendary or mythical tier is documented beyond the Alpha and Omega bosses and story-locked species (Helion, Lunara).

## Creature progression

### Takeaway
Creatures level up and evolve under per-species conditions. These combine a level, a stone, defeating that species' Alpha, a Sanctum clear, a personality letter or a story point. Stats come from a hidden Innate Potential (Common/Good/Elite/Perfect), Resonance upgrades, Potential Awakening, three rune slots, and an MBTI-style Personality. Eggs hatch on real-time timers; no true parent-pair breeding was found.

### Cited Findings
- **No single evolution level:** conditions are per species.
  - Iris → Irisal: Lv 18, defeat the Alpha Irisal, and 1 Sprout Stone.
  - Nimbi → Turbo: defeat the Alpha Turbo, Lv 21, and 3 Gust Stones.
  - Emberpup also needs a Sanctum clear.
  - Helion and Lunara are story-locked.
  - Gold arrows on a portrait mean the Aniimo is ready, grey means partly met.
  - Source: [search summary of Prima Games / KeenGamer / games.gg guides](https://primagames.com/tips/aniimo-evolution-guide-how-to-evolve-aniimo)
- Evolving most Aniimo needs rare materials. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- A Handbook-exclusive material drives a Handbook-exclusive Aniimo's branching evolution. — [search summary](https://games.gg/aniimo/guides/aniimo-evolution-how-to-evolve-every-creature/)
- **Stat growth:**
  - Standard levelling with evolution at set levels.
  - "Resonance Upgrades" raise base stats.
  - "Potential Awakening" raises skill levels and adds abilities.
  - Three-slot rune equipment.
  - Source: [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)
- **Innate Potential:**
  - Common → Good → Elite → Perfect, with Perfect about 0.8% from a wild catch.
  - The rating is a label over hidden totals (example: 32 for Perfect vs 30 for Elite), so it is IV-like.
  - Source (unverified): [aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)
- **Personality:**
  - Four MBTI letter pairs (E/I, S/N, T/F, J/P), each giving +2–6% combat.
  - Also gives a home-work bonus.
  - Some evolutions need a specific letter (Geoclaw, Melloblum).
  - Rerolled with a Psyche Fruit.
  - Source (unverified): [aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)
- **Skills:** each Aniimo has two primary skills, passives and traits, and an ultimate, run on cooldowns and energy. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
  - A shared team EP pool of six orbs; Ultimate Points in three segments. — [aniimotools.dev](https://aniimotools.dev/help/combat/)
- **Bonding:** feeding and bonding improves performance and unlocks bonuses. — [HostedGG](https://hostedgg.com/blog/aniimo-beginners-guide)
- **Eggs:**
  - Eggs are incubated on real-time timers in a "Hatchinator" at the Outpost or in the Homeland; the Homeland one is a mission reward.
  - Each habitat egg hatches one fixed species in about 10 minutes (Grassland → Emberpup, Forest → Hummin, Beach → Chirpi, Blossom → Iris, Highland → Fentuft).
  - An "Incredible" version of each egg gives Elite+ potential.
  - Source: [search summary of allthings.how / games.gg](https://allthings.how/aniimo-how-to-hatch-eggs-at-the-hatchinator/)
  - Sources disagree on the Sparkling Egg's hatch time (1 min vs 5 h).
- **Prismana Orb:** turns an owned Aniimo into its Prismana form, but only if you already own that species' Prismana. It keeps potential and personality, and a Morph Orb reverts it. Sources include the Stamp Shop, quests, and Lumin Crystal shop bundles (1,980–3,280 crystals). — [aniimo.kr (unverified)](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)

### Inferences
- **Evolve after beating your species' Alpha:** progression hangs on the world's boss. This is portable to Dexora as "defeat or catch the map's Alpha of the line".
- **Personality as a mild modifier:** a cosmetic-plus axis like Dexora's gender (derived, small), but Aniimo lets it gate evolution.

### Gaps
- No evidence of two-parent breeding or inheritance.
- The level cap for Aniimo is not found.

## Open world and exploration

### Takeaway
The world is one seamless world (Idyll) with a sky-city hub (Astra). It is split into regions and zones with level bands; Breezy Plains is the first, with 14 regions plus Whisperwake Isles counted for Nurture. Traversal is done by Twining into creatures, with elemental puzzles, Underground Palaces (dungeons), weather and day-night spawns, and a camper-van home.

### Cited Findings
- Grasslands, forests, aquatic areas and vertical terrain. Puzzles use fire, water and wind, and some have multiple solutions. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
- **Traversal slots:**
  - You can equip Aniimo as climber, flier and swimmer, and you auto-Twine into them when needed.
  - They need not be in the battle party.
  - Fast travel exists.
  - Aniimo clear paths, for example burning branches.
  - Source: [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- **Field abilities and dungeons:**
  - Some Aniimo dig, burn vines, collect Radiant Amber, or make tornadoes to move items.
  - Some areas and mini-games admit only a specific form.
  - "Underground Palaces" hold dungeon puzzles.
  - Source: [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)
- **Twine-gated content:** some quests and hidden areas need you Twined as a matching type to talk to NPCs. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- **Homeland:**
  - A camper van parks at campsites.
  - At home you plant crops, build houses, decorate and host Aniimo. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
  - Recruited Aniimo work the fields and machines, and setups can be fully automated. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
  - The RV park is a shared social hub. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- **Weather:** weather is regional and changeable, both by player intervention and through Prismana Flow. — [AniimoVerse (unverified)](https://www.aniimoverse.com/guides/prismana)

### Inferences

## Battles

### Takeaway
Combat is real time, with a party of four. You choose Command mode (the creature fights and you support or direct it) or Twine mode (you become the creature: dodge, skills, ultimates). Fights use elemental counters, a BREAK stagger gauge, and roles (DPS / BREAK / Support / REGEN / Healing). PvE covers Alpha and Omega bosses, Sanctums and seven regional "Assessments". PvP covers Egg Heist (PvPvE, three-player teams) and Twine-mode duels.

### Cited Findings
- **Two control styles:**
  - Command Mode coordinates the team. Twine Mode merges you with the Aniimo and gives you its attacks and movement.
  - Elemental matchups matter, especially against Alphas.
  - Source: [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
- **The protagonist in combat:**
  - When separated, the protagonist buffs and heals and goes down after about five hits.
  - Twine zooms the camera into a one-on-one action fight suited to elites and bosses.
  - You field four Aniimo, one at a time.
  - Element examples: fire > grass, grass > ground.
  - Source: [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)
- More element examples: Water > Fire; Light and Magic are each super-effective against the other. — [Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)
- **Modes and systems** (all from [aniimotools.dev](https://aniimotools.dev/help/combat/)):
  - Solo mode: switch freely among four.
  - Team mode: the lead fights and the others give brief support.
  - Command behaviours: Attack, Catch, Defense.
  - Elemental debuff stacks up to 20, and the weather adds debuffs.
  - Chain Ultimates between multiple players trigger a "Chain Burst".
  - Operation: Egg Heist: PvP with up to six parties, plus a PvE team mode.
  - "Pathfinder Challenge": Twine-mode duels with badge challenges.
- Egg Heist is PvPvE: three-player teams loot the Lost Islands against AI enemies and other players. Online co-op, PvP, crossplay and cross-progression are supported. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
- Raid bosses are mentioned in previews. — [search summary, Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/aniimo-hands-on-preview-a-pocket-monster-mash-up-of-everything-thats-come-before-1667163/)

### Inferences
- **Egg Heist:** a short competitive mode whose prize is rare eggs (Umbral, Shadow Sparkling). It ties PvP to collection rather than rating.

### Gaps

- The full element chart was not found.

## Distinctive mechanics vs Pokemon and Palworld

### Takeaway
Three mechanics set it apart:
- **Twine:** become your creature for combat, traversal and Twine-only NPCs and areas.
- **Completion-gated rare spawns:** regional catch % → badges → weather control and Prismana Flow.
- **Layered variants:** colour styles × forms × Umbral × potential × personality, with a stacking table.

### Cited Findings
- Previews single out Twine as the core hook. It turns Pokemon's command formula into real-time play as the creature. — [search summary, Gamereactor / Gamemeca](https://www.gamereactor.eu/aniimo-hands-on-preview-a-pocket-monster-mash-up-of-everything-thats-come-before-1667163/)
  - Twine in the open world gives gliding, diving, digging and climbing. — [game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)
