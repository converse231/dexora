# Limited-time event Pokémon with custom art in fan and browser Pokémon games

Researched 2026-10-19. Several primary sources returned 403 to the fetcher: delugerpg.com, delugerpg.net, pokefarm.wiki, pokefarm.com forum and wiki.pokerogue.net. For those sites, the claims below come from search-engine snippets of the named pages, and each one is flagged where it applies. Sources date from 2014 to 2025, and none of them confirms anything as "current as of October 2026" unless it says so.

## DelugeRPG: event Pokémon, how they are obtained, art and tradeability

### Takeaway
DelugeRPG event Pokémon are claimed by completing a task on weekends, holidays and site birthdays. They cannot be traded, and they get about 40% more battle EXP. I found nothing showing that DelugeRPG draws custom holiday art. Its sprites come from Smogon sprite projects and one community contributor.

### Cited Findings
- A forum guide says that "during weekends, holidays/festivals, and special occasions such as DelugeRPG's birthday, Christmas and New Year, there is an Event Pokemon that you can claim." You claim it by finishing a task, such as catching Pokémon of a set type or battling certain trainers. The guide is about 650 days old, so it dates from around early 2025. Search snippet only, because the page returned 403. — [How to Claim Event Pokemon, DelugeRPG Forums](https://www.delugerpg.net/viewtopic.php?t=30539)
- "Event Pokemon cannot be traded, but when used in battles, they receive about 40% more exp compared to non-event Pokemon." Search snippet. — [same thread](https://www.delugerpg.net/viewtopic.php?t=30539)
- The changelog says that "similar to event pokemon, G-Maxed pokemon are not tradeable", which confirms the no-trade rule from a second page. Snippet. — [DelugeRPG Updates](https://www.delugerpg.com/updates)
- An older holiday post (about 1,000 days old, so around 2024) raised the encounter chances of Legendary and "Special" Pokémon until New Year's Day. Holidays therefore also run boosted spawns, not only claims. Snippet. — [Holidays and New Year Event, DelugeRPG Forums](https://www.delugerpg.net/viewtopic.php?t=29255)
- The event page itself shows only "There is an active event!" with no details when not logged in. — [DelugeRPG Event](https://www.delugerpg.com/event)
- Art sources: the changelog credits sprite updates and all Gen 8 sprites to "various Sprite Projects at Smogon". Gen 9 sprites came from one contributor, whom the admin thanked by name: "Once again thanks to KingOfThe-X-Roads for the sprites." — [DelugeRPG Updates](https://www.delugerpg.com/updates); [Game Update: Gen 9 - Part 2](https://www.delugerpg.net/viewtopic.php?t=29486)
- Community sprite submissions are expected to follow Smogon's guidelines: 96x96px with a limited palette (about 15 colours). — [Sprite Creation thread](https://www.delugerpg.net/viewtopic.php?t=28190)
- A January 2025 forum reply said there are no plans to add animated sprites. — [Pokemon Animated Sprites thread](https://www.delugerpg.net/viewtopic.php?p=126772)

### Inferences
- DelugeRPG's model is "claim one, keep one, cannot trade". The 40% EXP perk makes event Pokémon useful as well as collectible. Its rarity comes from an ordinary species flagged as an event Pokémon, not from new art. That matches Dexora's rule that a variant is a look, never a strength, except that DelugeRPG's EXP perk is a strength.
- The no-trade rule removes event Pokémon from the market. That protects their meaning as "you were there", but it also means a missed event can never be made up.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm that DelugeRPG has any custom holiday-costume sprites. The pages that would say so (the event page, the Pokédex and the updates log) returned 403.
- How event Pokémon appear in the DelugeRPG Pokédex afterwards (a separate entry or a flag) is unverified.
- Exact cadence: the guide implies weekly weekend events, but I found no list of them.

## PokéFarm Q: Exclusives, Variants and Custom Sprites

### Takeaway
PokéFarm Q splits event art into two kinds. A Variant has a Pokédex entry and hatches from an egg. A Custom Sprite is a look only, gives no Pokédex entry, and is handed out at the end of holiday events or sold in event shops. Birthday customs are locked to the owner, while others trade and become collector's items.

### Cited Findings
- "Variants are Exclusive Pokémon that are based on another Pokémon. The difference to a Custom Sprite is that Variants have a Pokédex entry and hatch from an Egg." Search snippet, because the wiki returned 403. — [Exclusive Pokémon, PokéFarm Q Wiki](https://pokefarm.wiki/Exclusive_Pok%C3%A9mon)
- "Custom Sprites can be obtained through Events and promotions; however, they do not count towards the Pokédex as unique entries." Snippet. — [Custom Sprite, PokéFarm Q Wiki](https://pokefarm.wiki/Custom_Sprite)
- Custom Sprite Pokémon "can never be Shiny, Albino, or Melanistic, with the exception of Nerdy Illumise, show no gender specific differences and cannot be bred." In other words, the custom look replaces the colour tiers rather than stacking with them. Snippet. — [Custom Sprite wiki](https://pokefarm.wiki/Custom_Sprite)
- Some custom sprites were sold "at a Contest Hall for 500 for a limited time from 25th May 2015." Snippet. — [Custom Sprite wiki](https://pokefarm.wiki/Custom_Sprite)
- Birthday Pokémon "are locked, and cannot be traded to other users." Of older customs, a forum post says they are collector's items: "If you want one for yourself, you'll have to trade with someone for it." Snippets. — [Custom Sprite wiki](https://pokefarm.wiki/Custom_Sprite); [Custom Sprites forum thread](https://pokefarm.com/forum/thread/267232/Custom-Sprites/post/5319544)
- Halloween: the 2024 "Party Panic" event ended with each user getting one Custom Sprite Gengar. The 2025 "Heated Hallows" event gave a Halloween Supplies costume instead. Snippet. — [Events/Hallowe'en, PokéFarm Q Wiki](https://pokefarm.wiki/Events/Hallowe'en)
- Valentine's 2026 ("Watery Woes", 14–20 February) let players exchange "Sweet Hearts, Cookies, Chocolate Meowstic Custom Sprites, and Valentine Solynx and Lunupine Custom Sprites" at the Sweet Heart Stand. This is a one-week window with an event currency and a shop. Snippet. — [Watery Woes, PokéFarm Q Wiki](https://pokefarm.wiki/Watery_Woes)
- Christmas is "usually an advent calendar". One year (the post is tied to a "bf25" code, so likely 2025) it was replaced by "12 days of Christmas … beginning December 25th and run[ning] into the New Year." Snippet. — [Black Friday & December Events](https://pokefarm.com/forum/thread/489294/Black-Friday-December-Events)
- Other seasonal events listed on the wiki include April Fools, Valentine's and Bountiful Bouquet. — [April Fools](https://pokefarm.wiki/Events/April_Fools); [Valentine's Day](https://pokefarm.wiki/Valentine's_Day); [Bountiful Bouquet](https://pokefarm.wiki/Bountiful_Bouquet)
- On player-made art, a moderator said ownership and legal issues make player-made sprites hard to allow, and suggested players commission artists for display art in descriptions instead. — [special custom sprites? thread](https://pokefarm.com/forum/thread/182260/special-custom-sprites/post/3991206)
- The site also held custom sprite auctions. The thread title is all that was seen. — [Custom Sprite Auction! Part 2!](https://pokefarm.com/forum/thread/302131/Custom-Sprite-Auction-Part-2/post/5842939)

### Inferences
- PokéFarm's split between a dex entry (a Variant) and no dex entry (a Custom Sprite) is the clearest precedent for Dexora's choice. If event looks go in the Pokédex, a missed event leaves a permanent hole, which is FOMO. If they are a look only, as Dexora's "look" or "female" cells are, completionists are not punished.
- A guaranteed one-per-player gift at the end of an event, plus an event-currency shop for extra customs, is the recurring PokéFarm pattern.

### Gaps
- Who draws PokéFarm's customs (staff or a contest) and how many there are per event: the wiki pages that would say returned 403, so this is unverified.
- Whether old customs are ever re-released, beyond trading between players, is unconfirmed.

## Pokémon Vortex: themed variants on timed spawns, claims and minigames

### Takeaway
Vortex has run themed Pokémon since 2014, such as Christmas Caterpie, Star Wars Pikachu (Jedi) and Cubone (Vader), and Halloween Togepi. It uses every delivery route: timed map spawns, 24-hour login prizes, promo codes, battles, minigames, hatch events and auctions. Some return in later years.

### Cited Findings
- Christmas Caterpie spawned on grass maps at night in 2014 and ended 27 December 2014. It returned 19 December 2020 on Routes 14 and 15, with "both evolutions … time-limited like Caterpie (Christmas)'s spawns", ending 27 December 2020. — [Events, Pokémon Vortex Wiki](https://wiki.pokemon-vortex.com/wiki/Events)
- Star Wars Day (4 May) is a recurring series: Pikachu (Jedi) by promo code (4 May 2016), Scrafty (Ackbar) as a 24-hour daily login prize (4 May 2020), and Barbaracle (Grievous) on 4 May 2021. Cubone (Vader), Psyduck (Jar Jar) and Voltorb (BB-8) belong to the same set. — [Vortex Events wiki](https://wiki.pokemon-vortex.com/wiki/Events)
- Delivery mechanics on that page include:
  - Togepi (Halloween), claimed from the dashboard on 31 October–1 November 2016.
  - Rotom (Halloween), won by battling trainer "Creeper" on 30 October–2 November 2015 and re-released as a claim on 31 October–1 November 2017.
  - Diglett (Christmas), from a slot machine on 15–27 December 2016.
  - Pichu (Christmas), from Christmas Hatchathon eggs on 5 December 2018–7 January 2019, which could be bought and sold on PokéBay.
  - Pikachu (Christmas), which required owning the matching Pikachu variant as original trainer, 22–31 December 2015, and was re-run in February 2016 after server issues.
  - Pikachu Cosplay, five forms, 2–13 January 2015.

  — [Vortex Events wiki](https://wiki.pokemon-vortex.com/wiki/Events)
- Windows are typically 1 to 14 days. Example: Arceus (Unknown) appeared only on 1 April 2018, via a Magikarp Splash minigame. — [Vortex Events wiki](https://wiki.pokemon-vortex.com/wiki/Events)
- Monthly "Roamer of the Month" events run from the 1st to the last day of the month in UTC. Example: Enamorus (Therian), November 2024. — [Roaming Pokémon, Vortex Wiki](https://wiki.pokemon-vortex.com/wiki/Roaming_Pok%C3%A9mon)
- A fan project that clones Vortex lists six variant families: Normal, Shiny, Dark, Mystic, Metallic and Shadow. This is a fan GitHub repository, not official. — [Pokemon-Vortex-Custom GitHub](https://github.com/DigitalCure91/Pokemon-Vortex-Custom-WebBrowser-MMO)

### Inferences
- Vortex shows that event Pokémon can be reissued, as with Caterpie 2014 to 2020 and Rotom 2015 to 2017, and that a short window (one day, or the 4 May series) can become an annual ritual players look forward to.
- Requiring a prior event Pokémon to get a new one, as Christmas Pikachu 2015 did, compounds FOMO for anyone who missed the earlier event.

### Gaps
- The wiki does not say whether Vortex event Pokémon are tradeable, though Hatchathon eggs were sold on PokéBay. It also does not say who draws the themed sprites. The page text after about 2023 was not read.

## Pokémon Revolution Online (PRO): "Counterparts", accessorised or recoloured event forms

### Takeaway
PRO's seasonal events bring dozens of "specially accessorized and/or colorated" forms, 96 in Valentine's 2024 alone. They are hunted in the wild at about 1/550 (2018–19), have no stat effect and become tradeable collector commodities. Players' main complaint is unpredictable scheduling and dead time between events, not the forms themselves.

### Cited Findings
- Events recur annually: Valentine's (2018–2026), Easter (2018–2026), Summer (2020–2025/26), Halloween (2017–2025), Christmas (2018–2025), May 4th, April Fools, Bidoof Day and Starter Clones (2016). — [Pokémon Counterparts, PRO Wiki](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Pok%C3%A9mon_Counterparts)
- Valentine's Day 2024 gave "a total of 96 Pokémon" a counterpart. — [PRO Counterparts](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Pok%C3%A9mon_Counterparts)
- Rates and value:
  - Event forms in 2018–2019 (Valentine's and Easter) were 1/550 in the wild; later rates were not revealed.
  - Pink (Pinkan) forms are about 1/1,500; shinies are 1/8192.
  - Starter Clones were about 1/20 on wild starters.
  - Counterparts "do not improve the stats".
  - Event forms "were considered collectible commodities among traders".

  — [PRO Counterparts](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Pok%C3%A9mon_Counterparts)
- Art spans recolours, accessories, redesigns and new forms. Examples are the 2022 Ralts-line forms that varied by the Poké Ball used and the 2024 sprite reworks of the Bagon, Goomy and Magnemite lines. Starter Clones copy the clone colours from the Mewtwo movie. — [PRO Counterparts](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Pok%C3%A9mon_Counterparts)
- Returns: each year usually brings a new batch, but older sprites were sometimes huntable again (Valentine's 2020 and 2021, Easter 2020), and Starter Clones are now bundled into Halloween. — [PRO Counterparts](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Pok%C3%A9mon_Counterparts)
- The 2016 Christmas event released "specially accessorized and/or colorated Pokémon versions" at about 1/750. The 2023 Halloween event added "an unprecedented amount of forms". Search snippet. — [Official events, PRO Wiki](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Official_events)
- Halloween 2024 ran 11 October–10 November and offered a shiny Halloween Scyther through a hunting contest judged in Murky Town. — [Halloween Scyther Event 2024](https://pokemonrevolution.net/forum/topic/244036-halloween-scyther-event-2024/)
- Christmas opens a temporary map, Evergreen Island, reached by talking to Sailor Drew in any harbour city. Seasonal Pokémon are also wild everywhere except Pinkan Island. — [Christmas Event Guides](https://pokemonrevolution.net/forum/topic/248345-christmas-event-guides/)
- Free Safari events can include event forms, some of them membership-exclusive. Specifics are announced on Discord. — [PRO Official events](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Official_events)
- Player sentiment comes from a 2025 suggestion thread:
  - A player asked for a fixed annual calendar, calling the gaps between events "dead time" that drives players away.
  - They missed Ursaluna Bloodmoon because there was no clear Discord announcement.
  - Another player wanted events capped at one month, argued firm dates are risky, and asked for an outbreak icon showing what can be hunted.
  - Replies were split, with roughly two in favour and two against.

  Anecdotal, from four posters. — [Annual routine of events](https://pokemonrevolution.net/forum/topic/272897-annual-routine-of-events/)

### Inferences
- PRO is the closest analogue to a wild event catch: an event map plus event forms appended to wild tables at a low per-encounter rate. That maps onto Dexora's "append like legendaries and costumes" pipeline.
- Clear announcement and a predictable calendar matter as much as the content. Missing an event because you were not told is the sharpest grievance found.

### Gaps
- Who draws PRO's counterparts. The rest of the Counterparts page (about 205k characters) was not read.

## PokeMMO, PokéRogue and Pokémon Showdown

### Takeaway
PokeMMO's Halloween (yearly since 2015) is about trainer cosmetics ("vanities") from gift shops and mystery boxes, not costumed Pokémon. PokéRogue's events are rate boosts, such as 2x wild shiny, plus new shiny variant art that stays in the game permanently.

### Cited Findings
- PokeMMO Halloween has run every year since 2015. Rewards are vanities and particles, for example the untradeable Mage's Hat (2021) and new vanities (2023). Raids were added in 2024. — [Halloween, PokeMMO Wiki](https://pokemmo.shoutwiki.com/wiki/Halloween)
- The 2025 guide lists cosmetics by source: the Gift Shop, Goodie Bag drops and Halloween Mystery Boxes, each with a rarity tier (seasonal or rare). Snippet. — [Halloween 2025 guide, PokeMMO forums](https://forums.pokemmo.com/index.php?%2Ftopic%2F192700-halloween-2025-the-ultimate-halloween-event-guide%2F=)
- PokéRogue Halloween 2024 had "2x Rate of Wild Shiny Encounters in all modes" and 2x candy friendship in Classic. Its new shiny variants are "planned to be definitive" for those Pokémon. Halloween 2025 doubled wild shiny chances and raised the shiny catch-rate bonus to 3x. Snippets, because the wiki returned 403. — [Halloween 2024, PokéRogue Wiki](https://wiki.pokerogue.net/events:halloween2024); [Events overview](https://wiki.pokerogue.net/events:events)
- PokéRogue shiny events "occur infrequently and not on a consistent schedule". Snippet. — [Shiny Pokémon and Luck](https://wiki.pokerogue.net/gameplay:shiny_pokemon_and_luck)

### Inferences
- PokéRogue's pattern of new art launching in an event and then staying permanent avoids FOMO entirely: the event is the debut, not the only window.

### Gaps
- I did not research whether Pokémon Showdown has seasonal sprites, and I found nothing on it.

## Pokémon GO: costumed Pokémon as the official model

### Takeaway
Pokémon GO started costumes with Santa Hat Pikachu in December 2016, which could evolve to Raichu and keep its hat. Since then, many costumes are locked from evolving, and the rules are inconsistent. That lock, plus costumes appearing constantly, is the main source of player resentment. Costumes often return in later years.

### Cited Findings
- Santa Hat Pikachu arrived with the December 2016 update as a "special edition, limited time only Pikachu", catchable through about 29 December. Evolving it gave a Raichu that kept the hat. — [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/pokemon-go-update-adds-limited-edition-pikachu-wearing-santa-hat-2459083); [Forbes, 22 December 2016](https://www.forbes.com/sites/insertcoin/2016/12/22/heres-what-happens-when-you-evolve-santa-pikachu-in-pokemon-go/); [Serebii Christmas 2016](https://www.serebii.net/pokemongo/events/christmas2016.shtml)
- Bulbapedia now calls these "Event Pokémon" (formerly "Costume Pokémon"), available for a limited time during events. They are listed as form differences, not separate dex species. — [List of Pokémon with form differences in Pokémon GO, Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_with_form_differences_in_Pok%C3%A9mon_GO) (search snippet)
- Accessories tie into other releases. Examples: a TCG blue-cap Pikachu for a card-set crossover (16–30 June), and the Sinnoh starters in Lucas and Dawn's hats for the BDSP event, which ran alongside a collection challenge. — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/pokemon-gos-next-big-thing-is-pikachu-wearing-a-hat); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/pokemon/pokemon-go-brilliant-diamond-shining-pearl-event-guide)
- Evolution locks:
  - Fashion Week 2023 Kirlia "can't evolve into Gardevoir or Gallade". Player quotes include "I never understood why they would make an event costume Pokemon and not allow it to evolve" and "At least give me the opportunity to remove the stupid hat."
  - Critics call costumes low-effort filler released while features are missing. Others enjoy the "cute clothing".

  — [Dexerto, 21 November 2023](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-go-players-slam-pointless-customed-pokemon-2393411/)
- A blogger called the evolve rules "all wildly convoluted". Examples: Gengar has several costumes while Gastly and Haunter have none, and Butterfree has a bow that Caterpie and Metapod lack. Sunglasses Squirtle returned and could evolve. This is a personal blog. — [spuf.org, November 2022](https://spuf.org/2022/11/04/another-ramble-about-costumed-pokemon/)
- A September 2022 r/PokemonGO thread, "Anyone else not care about costume Pokemon?", said costumes "used to be fun at the beginning" but became "a nuisance", with poor IVs and little battle use. As reported by Sportskeeda. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/pokemon/news-anyone-else-care-costume-pokemon-the-pokemon-go-community-discusses-efficacy-dressed-up-monsters)
- A resale market for event costumes exists, with eBay listings of costumed Pokémon "for trade". — [eBay listing example](https://www.ebay.com/itm/387583274969)
- Pokémon GO Halloween 2026 is scheduled for Part 1, 27 October–1 November, and Part 2, 1–5 November. — [Serebii GO events](https://www.serebii.net/pokemongo/events.shtml); [Pokémon GO Hub](https://pokemongohub.net/post/event/halloween-2026-part-one/)

### Inferences
- The costume's novelty wears off when costumes are frequent and the costumed Pokémon is a dead end: it cannot evolve, battles poorly and clutters storage. The first costume was loved partly because it was rare and still evolved.
- For Dexora, an event look should travel through evolution the way tiers do, or simply be a look on a normal Pokémon. Otherwise it repeats GO's most-cited annoyance.

### Gaps
- An official Niantic statement of the costume evolution rule. Only fan observations were found.
- Counts of costumes per year, and trade restrictions on costumes. Not found from a fetchable source.

## Cross-game patterns: mechanics, sentiment and art

### Takeaway
There are four delivery models:
1. A guaranteed claim or gift, one per player (DelugeRPG, PokéFarm's end-of-event custom, Vortex dashboard claims).
2. A low-rate wild form on event maps or appended tables (PRO at about 1/550 to 1/750, Vortex timed spawns, GO).
3. An event-currency shop (PokéFarm's Sweet Heart Stand, PokeMMO's gift shop).
4. A rate boost with permanent new art (PokéRogue).

Windows run from one day to one month, most commonly 1 to 2 weeks. Annual recurrence with occasional re-releases is the norm.

### Cited Findings
- Window lengths:
  - Vortex: 1 to 14 days.
  - PokéFarm Valentine's 2026: 7 days.
  - PRO Halloween 2024: 31 days.
  - GO Halloween 2026: about 9 days in two parts.

  — sources above
- Tradeability splits:
  - Not tradeable: DelugeRPG event Pokémon, PokéFarm birthday customs, PokeMMO's Mage's Hat.
  - Tradeable collectibles: PRO counterparts and older PokéFarm customs.

  — sources above
- Pokédex treatment:
  - PokéFarm: a Custom Sprite gets no dex entry, a Variant gets one.
  - GO: costumes are form differences.
  - PRO: counterparts are cosmetic, with no stat change.

  — sources above
- Art scale: PRO did 96 forms in one Valentine's event, while PokéFarm and DelugeRPG give one or a few Pokémon per event. Smogon-style sprites are 96x96 with about 15 colours. — [PRO Counterparts](https://wiki.pokemonrevolution.net/index.php?title=Pok%C3%A9mon_Counterparts); [DelugeRPG Sprite Creation](https://www.delugerpg.net/viewtopic.php?t=28190)

### Inferences
- What players resent:
  - Missing an event because of poor announcement (PRO).
  - Event Pokémon that cannot evolve (GO).
  - Too many costumes, so novelty fades (GO).
  - Dead time between events (PRO).
- What they praise or keep: annual rituals (Vortex's 4 May, PRO's yearly batches), re-runs, and event Pokémon that keep their value as tradeable commodities.
- A design that fits Dexora's rules:
  - Treat an event look as a "look" (no new dex position, so it is safe for saves keyed by position).
  - Let it ride evolution.
  - Announce it through News and the HUD's event card.
  - Give each event one guaranteed claim plus a low-rate wild chase.
  - Allow re-runs in later years so that missing one is not permanent.

### Gaps
- Who draws event art (staff, contests or commissions) is undocumented for every game here except DelugeRPG's base sprites (Smogon projects and one named contributor). PokéFarm's moderators discourage player-made sprites for legal reasons.
- No quantitative data on how often events can run before fatigue sets in. Only anecdotes.
