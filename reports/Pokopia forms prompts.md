# Pokopia forms: sprite prompts

Seven special forms from Pokémon Pokopia, drawn as our own Gen 3 sprites.

**How to use**
- Start a new Gemini chat for every Pokémon.
- Attach TWO images, in this order: first the reference sprite named beside it (the game's own sprite: pixel size, outline and shading), then the official Pokopia artwork of that form (the design and colours). The prompts refer to them as the first and second image.
- Save each result at the path beside its name. `npm run eventart` leaves the folder alone until these are built into the game.
- If Gemini drifts from the artwork, reply in the same chat: "follow the second image's design and colours exactly, at the first image's pixel size".
- Peakychu's body is cream: if the result comes back with a body as pale as the background, reply "make the body a warmer ivory cream, clearly darker than the white background, with a dark brown outline all round".

```
Pixel art in the style of a Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen front battle sprite (Game Boy Advance, Gen 3): a LOW-RESOLUTION pixel sprite about the size of the first attached image, shown blown up so each pixel is a big 16x16 square block - big chunky pixels like that reference, never fine detail. Crisp 1-pixel dark outlines, flat shading with two or three tones per colour, a small limited palette, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no smooth curves. This is Professor Tangrowth, a special form of Tangrowth from Pokémon Pokopia. The second attached image is its official artwork: copy its design, colours and details exactly. The first attached image is the game's ordinary Tangrowth sprite: match its pixel size, outline weight and shading style, and keep its facing. Its body is Tangrowth's round mass of blue vines hanging down to the ground, but the vines on top of its head have turned white and curl into big round coils like a professor's wild white hair. From the very top rises one long thin white vine that curls over at the tip and holds a small shiny silver disc with a faint rainbow shine. In the dark gap of its face are its two white eyes with black pupils, and just below them sits a pair of small round wire-rimmed spectacles with silver lenses. Its two long blue vine arms end in rosy red-pink hands: one arm is raised high, holding a long thin wooden pointer stick that forks into two prongs at its tip; the other hand rests low at its side. Its feet are dark grey-black. Give it no arms, hands or legs it does not have in the artwork. Any white in it is a soft cream or a pale grey, never pure white, so it never looks like the background. Keep it compact so the whole sprite stays about the size of the first reference, nothing reaching past the edges of the frame. Full body, centered, with plenty of white space around it. Plain pure white background, no shadow, no text, no border, square 1024x1024.
```
