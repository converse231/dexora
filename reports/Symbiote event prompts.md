# Symbiote event: Gemini prompts

**How to use**
- Start a **new Gemini chat for every Pokémon**: Gemini reuses the last picture in a chat.
- Attach that Pokémon's reference image (each prompt names its file) from:
  `C:\Users\admin\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\c--Users-admin-Desktop-mini-games-pokemon-collect\51500524-1af5-450f-9f87-06a0893303a1\scratchpad\symbiote\`
- **Make Gengar first.** Attach your finished Gengar as image 2 to every other prompt, so the ooze looks the same on all of them.
- Save each result as `art/events/symbiote/<number>.jpg`, the number being the reference file's (`ref-6-charizard.png` → `6.jpg`). `npm run eventart` does the rest.

**Why the prompts are worded this way.** Gemini refused the first version ("interests of third-party content providers"): the words *symbiote*, *suit* and *spider emblem* together point it at a comic character, and it blocks that. These prompts describe the same look - glossy black, white jagged eyes, fanged grin, tendrils - as a **living black ooze**, the way the Halloween costumes were described. Keep it that way when you edit one: no comic names and no logos.

**Shape first** (2026-10-20: an all-black Spinarak grew tendrils that read as extra legs): every one is COMPLETELY black, but its outline and pose stay those of the reference - the ooze hugs the body like a skin, and a tendril never looks like an extra limb. That is what keeps it recognisable.

**Every stage is completely taken** (2026-10-20): a Charmander is as covered as its Charizard. What grows with evolution is the menace - small fangs and short tendrils on a first stage, the full grin, tongue and long tendrils on the last.

**Gengar is patient zero:** the ooze reached it first, on Deoxys's meteor, so it is completely covered, and so are its Mega and Gigantamax forms.
