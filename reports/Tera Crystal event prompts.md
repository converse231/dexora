# Tera Crystal - sample prompts (Stellar Eeveelutions)

Three samples to test the look before the week is built. The Eeveelutions are
Stellar Tera Pokémon ex in Prismatic Evolutions (`sv08.5`, already in the
game's cards).

## What a Terastallized Pokémon looks like (from the games and the cards)

1. The WHOLE body turns to cut crystal: a mosaic of flat triangular and
   polygon facets in three or four shades of the body's own colour, with white
   glints on some facets - stained glass, not a normal body with highlights.
2. A bright rim of light runs round the silhouette, with a faint rainbow
   sheen on the edges.
3. The crown is TWO pieces: a small crystal tiara sitting on the head, and a
   big Tera Jewel FLOATING above it with a clear gap. Each type has its own
   floating jewel (Electric a lightbulb, Psychic an eye, Fire a candelabra);
   STELLAR's is a halo ring of small hexagonal gems, one in each type's colour
   (the Umbreon ex card, sv8a 217), over a white spiky tiara.

The second try put the halo far above the head (Vaporeon's over its tail), so it now hovers low, centred over the tiara. The first try (2026-11-03) gave a normal Umbreon with highlights and one
rainbow spike cluster glued to its head - none of the three.

Attach TWO images to each prompt: the game's sprite enlarged
(`art/events/ref/197-umbreon-front.png`, `700-sylveon-front.png`,
`134-vaporeon-front.png`) as the base, and the Umbreon ex Special Art card as
the crown and facet reference.

**Stellar Tera Umbreon** → `art/events/tera/197.jpg`
```
Pixel art in the style of a Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen front battle sprite (Game Boy Advance, Gen 3): a LOW-RESOLUTION 64x64 pixel sprite, shown blown up so each pixel is a big 16x16 square block - big chunky pixels like the first attached image, never fine detail. Crisp 1-pixel outlines, flat colours, a limited palette, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no smooth curves. A Terastallized Umbreon, Stellar Tera type, as in the second attached image (the Umbreon ex card). Use the first attached Umbreon sprite as the exact base: the same pose, facing, proportions and pixel size. Terastallizing turns its WHOLE body into cut crystal, like stained glass: every part of the body - head, ears, legs, tail - is a mosaic of flat triangular and polygon facets, each facet 3 to 5 pixels across, in four shades of navy and dark blue with a few pale blue facets, and single white pixel glints on some facet corners. Its yellow rings stay yellow but become faceted gold glass, and its red eyes stay red. A thin bright outline of light runs around the whole silhouette, with a few rainbow-tinted pixels along its edge. On its head sits a small white spiky crystal tiara, its points tipped in rainbow colours. ABOVE its head floats a small halo of nine hexagonal gems, each 4 pixels wide and each a different colour - red, blue, yellow, green, pink, purple, cyan, orange and white - every gem with one lighter pixel in its centre. The halo is a flat oval seen slightly from the front, about three gems tall, centred directly over the tiara, and it hovers LOW: its bottom gems sit just 1 to 2 pixels above the tiara's points, without touching them, and its top is no higher than the tips of the ears. The halo is no wider than the head and ears together. Two or three tiny four-pointed sparkles around it. Full body, centered, with plenty of white space around it. Plain pure white background, no shadow, no text, no border, square 1024x1024.
```
