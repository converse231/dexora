# Red and Green beat waiting for Gen 10

Generation 10 will not reach PokeAPI before about **early 2028**. Pokémon Winds and Waves were announced on 27 February 2026 for a 2027 Switch 2 release with no date, only three new species (the starters Browt, Pombon and Gecqua) are revealed, and PokeAPI serves 1,025 species with no Gen 10 issue or pull request open. On the Gen 9 precedent, species data lands two to three weeks after launch and front sprites two to four months after, so Dexora can plan a Gen 10 append for Q1 2028. Until then the only new Pokémon are the Legends: Z-A and Champions Megas, already in PokeAPI as forms up to id 10326; Dexora already ships them to 10325 and lacks one, Meowstic's female Mega. The attached Japanese Red & Green sheet is exactly the idea behind the Origin tier, one step further back: Red and Green are the first drawings of every Kanto species, and Yellow, Dexora's current Kanto Origin, is a later redraw. PokeAPI already hosts the full set in Super Game Boy colours, on the host and in the folder pattern Dexora already fetches. That makes a "debut" tier of Red/Green (151) plus Gold/Silver (251) the best next build: official art, scriptable, clearly distinct, no new licence question. After it come the Gen 6 3D renders and the PMD Sprite Collab portraits. The fan sets with real licence or consent problems (Infinite Fusion, PokeRogue, Space World leaks) should be skipped.

## Gen 10 arrives in PokeAPI around Q1 2028, not before

Winds and Waves are set in a tropical archipelago modelled on Southeast Asia and are due "exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2 in a simultaneous worldwide release in 2027" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Winds_and_Waves); [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/gaming/news/pokemon-winds-waves-new-games-starters-release-date-1236674619/)). Of the 54 Pokémon confirmed from trailer imagery by late September, **only the three starters are new**; the rest return from earlier generations ([CBR](https://www.cbr.com/pokemon-winds-waves-reveals-54-pokemon-gen-10/)). Two dressed-up Pikachu, Mr. Windychu and Ms. Wavychu, are costumes, not species ([Nerdist](https://nerdist.com/article/pokemon-winds-waves-new-games-share-trailer-release-dates-and-starters/)). Leaks claim more than 300 new Pokémon and disagree on the month, September or "late 2027" ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/pokemon-winds-and-waves-release-pushed-to-late-2027-according-to-popular-leaker/); [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/leak-mentions-release-months-for-pokemon-winds-and-waves-and-new-3d-mario-2027)). None of it is confirmed. Every recent mainline generation launched in October or November, so September to November 2027 is the working estimate.

PokeAPI today reports **1,025 species and 1,351 Pokémon entries** ([pokeapi.co](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon-species?limit=1)). Its recent work is Champions sprites (#1701, merged 16 October 2026) and Gen 9 clean-up, and the only Winds and Waves mention in its tracker concerns Brazilian Portuguese as a future language ([GitHub issues](https://api.github.com/repos/PokeAPI/pokeapi/issues?state=all&per_page=40&sort=updated)). Nothing can be added early: three species with names and art but no stats are not a data set, and PokeAPI has never added pre-release reveals.

The precedents set the clock. After PokeAPI dropped its Veekun dependency it became fast. Scarlet and Violet launched on 18 November 2022, and **all new species with base stats merged on 4 December, 16 days later** ([PR 778](https://api.github.com/repos/PokeAPI/pokeapi/pulls/778)). Official artwork followed on 23 January 2023 and default front sprites by 5 February, with Showdown-style sprites in April ([sprites search](https://api.github.com/search/issues?q=repo:PokeAPI/sprites+gen+9+OR+paldea+OR+scarlet&sort=created&order=asc&per_page=40)). The Z-A Megas reached the API **one day after launch** ([PR 1317](https://api.github.com/repos/PokeAPI/pokeapi/pulls/1317)), but their sprites took two to three months. Sword and Shield are the slow outlier, about 14 months, because PokeAPI waited on CSVs that never came ([issue 460](https://api.github.com/repos/PokeAPI/pokeapi/issues/460/comments?per_page=50)). A generation of 100 or more species is the Gen 9 job, not the Z-A one. Since `npm run assets` needs both species data and front sprites, **Dexora's practical Gen 10 date is two to four months after launch, most likely Q1 2028**. Flavour text and learnsets run on long tails: Gen 9 gaps are still open in 2026. Under Dexora's own append-only rule, the new national ids (1026+) would sit after the existing forms in `SPECIES` order, not between 1025 and 10001.

Before then the only new Pokémon are Megas. The live API ends with golisopod-mega (10316) through meowstic-female-mega (10326), from Z-A, its Mega Dimension DLC and Pokémon Champions patches through 1.2 ([pokeapi.co](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon?offset=1340&limit=100); [PR 1701](https://api.github.com/repos/PokeAPI/pokeapi/pulls/1701)). A check of the repo's `src/data/forms.js` shows Dexora **already carries forms through 10325**, including Golisopod, Zeraora and Baxcalibur's Megas, and Meowstic's male Mega but not the female one. Re-running the form fetch should pick up 10326. Any further Champions patch will arrive the same way, a few forms at a time.

## The Red and Green sheet is the true Kanto origin, and PokeAPI already hosts it

Japanese Blue gave every Pokémon new front sprites, and international Red and Blue shipped Blue's art, not Red and Green's ([Bulbapedia, Pokémon Blue (Japanese)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Blue_(Japanese)); [TCRF](https://tcrf.net/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Blue/Version_Differences)). Red and Green is therefore the **first drawing of all 151 species**, and many of those fronts are famously odd and off-model. That is what a debut collectible should look like. Dexora's Origin for Kanto is Yellow, a third pass at the same art, so the user's sheet is the same concept as Origin, only older.

The set is one fetch away. `PokeAPI/sprites/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-i/red-green-japan/{id}.png` holds **151 files, ids 1-151**, with `back/` and `gray/` subfolders ([PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/tree/master/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-i)). The colour is the sheet's: Charizard's palette is `181010 d65231 ffa552 f7ffef`, four Super Game Boy-style shades, against Yellow's `ffffff d64a31 ffc55a 313131` and the saturated `ff0000 ff8c00` of Yellow's GBC folder ([raw file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-i/red-green-japan/6.png)). Canvases run 40-56px by species, so they need the same nearest-neighbour scaling Origin already uses. Pokémon Showdown serves the same art as `gen1rg/` (151 files, 96x96 pre-scaled, by species name) ([Showdown](https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/gen1rg/)). Bulbagarden Archives has it too but forbids hotlinking and answers scripts with a Cloudflare challenge ([Archives:Policy](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Archives:Policy)), so it is not a source.

Two things are unknown. PokeAPI does not document which SGB palette table its colours came from, and the list of which Blue fronts Yellow redrew could not be read. That second gap matters if Red/Blue were ever made a tier of its own, because Red/Blue and Yellow share much of their art. Red/Green against Yellow is a different matter: Blue redrew every front.

## Official sets: the debut art is strong, the base-era sets are not

The test for a tier is whether it reads as a different drawing on a 56px Box row, against FireRed, HeartGold, Black/White and PokeAPI's default renders. Measured on 19 October 2026, the PokeAPI folders sort cleanly ([PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites)):

| Set (PokeAPI folder) | Species covered | Format | Distinct from Dexora's base? |
|---|---|---|---|
| red-green-japan | 151 | PNG 40-56px, SGB colour | **Yes**: the debut art |
| gold, silver | 251 each (+Unown) | PNG, 56px | **Yes** for Johto; differs from Crystal (current Origin) for many species |
| ruby-sapphire, emerald | 386 | PNG 64px | Weak: largely FRLG's art |
| diamond-pearl, platinum | 493 | PNG 80px | Weak: Platinum redrew only a subset |
| x-y, omega-ruby-alpha-sapphire | 721 | PNG renders, 120px (ORAS) | **Yes**: 3D model renders, a different medium |
| ultra-sun-ultra-moon | 802 | animated GIF, ~86 KB each | Yes, but heavy |
| sword-shield through champions | partial per game, up to 1025 | WebP 512px | Yes, but no single game covers the dex |
| other/dream-world | 936 | SVG | Yes: flat vector Sugimori style |
| other/home | 1025 | PNG 512px | Weak: close to the default renders |
| other/official-artwork | 1025 | PNG 475px | A painting, not a sprite |

Three findings follow. First, a per-generation "debut" ladder is cheap and the Gen 2 rung is real: Gold, Silver and Crystal are three separate official drawings ([PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/tree/master/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-ii)). The Gen 3 and Gen 4 rungs are not, because Ruby/Sapphire and Diamond/Pearl are mostly the base art. Dexora already excludes Hoenn and Sinnoh from Origin for this reason, and the same reasoning applies. Second, the X/Y and ORAS renders cover 721 species, nearly 70% of the National Dex, in a look nothing in the game resembles. Dexora's default from Gen 6 is a PokeAPI render, so whether these renders separate visibly for 650-721 has to be checked by eye. Third, the README marks provenance that needs credit: Black/White sprites above #650 are Smogon customs, the Gen 9 fronts are KingOfThe-X-Roads', and the Let's Go-onward renders are FallenDeity's ([README](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites)).

Spin-off sets mostly fail on coverage or access. Picross covers 256-304 entries from Gens 1-6 and Shuffle about 267 files, and neither has a scriptable host ([Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_in_Pok%C3%A9mon_Picross); [TCRF](https://tcrf.net/Pok%C3%A9mon_Shuffle)). Pokémon GO's mined models cover most of the dex, but their host says "for educational use only... property of The Pokemon Company and Niantic" ([PokeMiners](https://github.com/PokeMiners/pogo_assets)), a stricter stance than PokeAPI's. The Space World '97 beta sprites come from a leak whose files were pulled after takedown pressure ([PokeCommunity Daily](https://daily.pokecommunity.com/2020/04/25/major-discoveries-revealed-from-pokemon-gold-and-silver-demo)). They are not released-game art, and they do not fit the README's stance. No complete scriptable rip was found for Ranger, Stadium, Pinball, Café, Masters or Sleep.

On licensing, no host grants rights over the art. PokeAPI's CC0 covers its own work, and the repo says "All image contents within are Copyright The Pokémon Company" ([LICENCE.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/LICENCE.txt)). For official sets, Dexora's safety is the same as it is today: non-commercial use, self-hosted files, credit for the rippers and renderers.

## Fan sets: PMD Collab is clean, the rest carry conditions or risk

**PMD Sprite Collab** is the strongest fan source. It covers **every entry from #0000 to #1025 with a portrait**, 962 with overworld sprites, and 2,080 form subgroups, by a count of its `tracker.json` ([tracker.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/master/tracker.json)). It is licensed **CC BY-NC 4.0**, which Dexora's no-ads, no-payments stance satisfies ([LICENSE.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/master/LICENSE.md)). Download is plain GitHub raw: `portrait/<dex4>/Normal.png` and `sprite/<dex4>/Idle-Anim.png` plus `AnimData.xml`. The repo was committed to on the day of research ([commits](https://api.github.com/repos/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/commits?per_page=1)). Credit is machine-readable: each species folder has `credits.txt`, and `credit_names.txt` maps IDs to names ([credits example](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/master/sprite/0025/credits.txt)), so a per-species credit line can be generated the way `SAGE_CREDIT` works. There are two catches. The art is a **40x40 bust** or a 40x56 top-down walker, not a front battle sprite, so it reads as a portrait card rather than the creature standing. And **492 of 1,026 portraits are Spike Chunsoft's official art**, not fan work, so the tier mixes official and community art and its credit must name Spike Chunsoft.

The **Smogon Sprite Project** (BW-style 96px for Gen 6-9) is free "for non-profit use" with credit, but its X/Y terms also say "You must make a credit page and link back to this thread" and "Please do not redistribute these sprites" ([X/Y thread](https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/x-y-sprite-project.3486712/); [Gen 8+ thread](https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/smogon-sprite-project.3647722/)). It is also stylistically the base: Dexora's Black/White default already includes these customs above #650, and the Showdown tier already uses the animated set. It adds nothing new and carries the strictest terms.

**pokeemerald-expansion's** `graphics/pokemon/<species>/front.png` gives 64x64 GBA-style art for the whole dex, and Dexora already pins the repo for animations and followers. Its Gen 4+ fronts come from PokeCommunity's "DS Style Gen VI" and "Gen VII and Beyond" resource threads ([CREDITS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/master/CREDITS.md)), which ask users to credit the thread ([PokeCommunity](https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/the-ds-style-64x64-pok%C3%A9mon-sprite-resource-completed.267728/post-7444245)). The exact terms could not be read (the site returned 403). For Gens 1-3 the art is the base art, so the tier is distinct only from Gen 4 on.

**SageDeoxys** permits use "so long as you inform me of its use, and credit me as the creator" ([spritesheet terms](https://art.cleberg.net/post/sagedeoxys/S-Meltan-Spritesheet-765371916)). Dexora already credits SageDeoxys; informing them is a separate stated condition and should be confirmed. No catalogue of SageDeoxys' other complete sets was found.

Three sets to avoid. **Infinite Fusion** publishes no reuse licence, and its artists withdrew work en masse in late 2024, deleted on request by an automated system ([Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/?p=1010168); [FAQ](https://infinitefusiondex.com/faq)). **PokeRogue's** assets are CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, but the art is largely Game Freak's and Smogon's ([pokerogue-assets](https://github.com/pagefaultgames/pokerogue-assets); [CREDITS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pagefaultgames/pokerogue/beta/CREDITS.md)). That licence is not PokeRogue's to grant over that art, and ShareAlike would bind Dexora's derivatives. Space World leaks are covered above.

## Ranked: build the debut tier, then decide on renders or portraits

| Rank | Tier | Source and download | Coverage | Fit | Licence and credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Debut: Red/Green (Kanto) + Gold or Silver (Johto) | PokeAPI raw, `generation-i/red-green-japan/{id}.png`, `generation-ii/gold/{id}.png` | 251 | Pixel art at Origin's scale; older than Origin | Official; same footing as Origin |
| 2 | 3D renders (X/Y or ORAS) | PokeAPI raw, `generation-vi/omega-ruby-alpha-sapphire/{id}.png` | 721 | A different medium; verify against the Gen 6 default | Official renders; credit PokeAPI |
| 3 | PMD portraits | GitHub raw, `portrait/<dex4>/Normal.png`; credits from `tracker.json` | 1,026 | Charming but a bust, not a body; 40px upscaled 2x | CC BY-NC 4.0; per-species artist credit, Spike Chunsoft for 492 |
| 4 | GBA-style (pokeemerald-expansion) | Existing pin, `graphics/pokemon/*/front.png` | Distinct only for Gen 4+ (~640) | Fits the Gen 3 base look | Credit three PokeCommunity threads; terms unverified |
| 5 | Dream World | PokeAPI `other/dream-world/{id}.svg` | 936 | Vector art clashes with pixelated scaling | Official |
| Skip | Smogon BW, HOME, RS/DP, Infinite Fusion, PokeRogue, GO, Space World | — | — | Same as the base, or the terms or consent fail | — |

Rank 1 wins on every axis. It reuses the Origin pipeline, art gates and pixel scaling, and only Kanto and Johto species can wear it, a lock `ART_GATES` already handles. One design choice comes first: make it a **new tier**, not a swap of Origin's Kanto art to Red/Green. Swapping would silently redraw every Kanto Origin a player already holds, and Yellow versus Red/Green is itself the variety. Name it carefully. "Red", "Green", "Ruby" and "Sapphire" collide with existing tier names (Fire Red, Leaf Green and the gem tiers), so a word like "Debut" or "Pocket" avoids eleven screens of confusion. Use `gray/` only if a monochrome tier is ever wanted. Rank 2 against rank 3 is a choice between coverage-safe official art and full coverage with a mixed credit pipeline. Pick the renders if the game should stay official-only, the portraits if a whole-dex tier matters more.

## Conclusion

Gen 10 is a 2028 task, and nothing gained by waiting changes what can be built now. The highest-value art is older than anything Dexora shows, not newer: the 1996 Red/Green drawings are the strongest new tier because they are both the oldest official art and the easiest to fetch. Until Winds and Waves launch, the to-do list is short. Fetch Meowstic's female Mega (10326) and re-run the form fetch after each Champions patch. Build the debut tier from PokeAPI. Confirm SageDeoxys has been told their art is in use. Then, once the launch date is set, schedule the Gen 10 append for about two to four months after it: species arrive within weeks, but `npm run assets` needs sprites, and those take a season. When the append comes, keep the new ids after the forms already shipped; the save format, not the dex number, decides where Gen 10 sits.
