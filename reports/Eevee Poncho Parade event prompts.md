# Eevee Poncho Parade: art prompts

The event runs **November 15 to 21, 2026**, the week after the Pikachu Poncho Parade. Eevee are out in ponchos, each on the maps its poncho's Pokémon would live on. The 8 printed on real cards (the 2017 Pokémon Center campaign, SM-P 137-144) give their promo card on the first catch. Catch 20 and **Mew in an Eevee poncho** comes back to the Safari Zone.

**How to use**
- Start a new Gemini chat for every Pokémon, and **attach the reference sprite** with the prompt: the game's Eevee for the ponchos - `art/events/ref/133-eevee-front.png`, already enlarged, with the stray red cross on its chest painted out (it is in the original FireRed sprite, and Gemini copies it) - the game's Mew (`public/sprites/151.png`, enlarged) for Mew. Every prompt is written around it.
- **For the 8 official ponchos, also attach the real card**: `public/cards/eponcho/hi/<card>/high.webp` (the card number is under each heading). Two images: the Eevee sprite for the pose and pixels, the card for the poncho.
- **Check before saving:** one tail, the reference's pose, chunky pixels, no brown Eevee ears poking out of the hood, nothing touching the edges. If it is off, reply in the same chat: "one tail only, Eevee's own ears hidden in the hood, the exact pose and pixel size of the reference, smaller costume".
- Save each result at the path above its prompt: `art/events/eponchos/<look id>.jpg`. These are named by **look id**, not species, because every one is an Eevee. Then run `npm run eventart`.
- Save the title badge as `art/titles/event-eponchos.png`, then run `npm run ranks`.

**The look.** Each one follows the official Poncho-wearing Eevee design:
- The **hood is the Pokémon's head**: its ears, fins, gem or ribbons sit on top, its eyes on the hood's brow, and Eevee's brown face peeks out through the front.
- **Eevee's own long ears are hidden** inside the hood; its cream chest and brown paws show under a short cape.
- A **collar** in the costume Pokémon's style (Vaporeon's frill, Jolteon's spikes, Flareon's fluff, Sylveon's ribbon bow).
- One small magical touch.

Every Eevee is the reference sprite redrawn with its poncho over it: the same pose, the same chunky pixels, one tail. That way all 20 read as one family with the game's own Eevee, and nothing is lost when the game shrinks it to its 64-pixel canvas.
