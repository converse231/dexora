# Poncho Parade: art prompts

The event runs **November 8 to 14, 2026**. Pikachu are out in the Pokémon Center's ponchos, each on the maps its poncho's Pokémon would live on. The 18 printed on real cards give their promo card on the first catch. Catch 20 and **Mew in a Pikachu poncho** comes out in the Safari Zone.

**How to use**
- Start a new Gemini chat for every Pokémon, and **attach the reference sprite** with the prompt: your Pikachu sprite for the ponchos, the game's Mew (`public/sprites/151.png`, enlarged) for Mew. Every prompt is written around it.
- **For the 22 official ponchos, also attach the official design** from `art/events/ref/ponchos/<look id>` (the Pokémon Center's own figure, keychain or card - Scizor is on `19958-19959.png`). Two images: your Pikachu for the pose and pixels, the official one for the poncho.
- **Check before saving:** one tail, the reference's pose, chunky pixels, nothing touching the edges. If it is off, reply in the same chat: "one tail only, the exact pose and pixel size of the reference, smaller costume".
- Save each result at the path above its prompt: `art/events/ponchos/<look id>.jpg`. These are named by **look id**, not species, because every one is a Pikachu. Then run `npm run eventart`.
- Save the title badge as `art/titles/event-ponchos.png`, then run `npm run ranks`.

**The look.** Each one follows the official Poncho-wearing Pikachu design:
- The **hood is the Pokémon's head**: its eyes and horns sit on top, and Pikachu's face peeks out through the front.
- A **cape** carries the Pokémon's wings, tails or shell.
- A **clasp** at the neck: a rainbow Mega Stone for the Mega ponchos, a Poké Ball for the two Vulpix.
- One small magical touch.

Every Pikachu is the reference sprite redrawn with its poncho over it: the same pose, the same chunky pixels, one tail. That way all 23 read as one family with the game's own Pikachu, and nothing is lost when the game shrinks it to its 64-pixel canvas.

**Reference.** The real cards are in the game now at `public/cards/poncho/` (open `hi/<card>/high.webp` for the big scan). Drag one into the Gemini chat with its prompt for the closest match.

Pixel art in the style of a Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen front battle sprite (Game Boy Advance, Gen 3): a LOW-RESOLUTION 64x64 pixel sprite, the whole body about 55 pixels tall, shown blown up so each pixel is a big 16x16 square block - big chunky pixels like the reference, never fine detail. Crisp 1-pixel dark outlines, flat shading with two or three tones per colour, a small limited palette, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no smooth curves. Pikachu wearing a hooded poncho. Use the attached Pikachu sprite as the exact base: the same pose, the same facing, the same body proportions, the same chunky pixel size and the same yellow palette - redraw it pixel for pixel and only add the poncho over it. The sprite has EXACTLY ONE TAIL: Pikachu's own yellow zig-zag lightning-bolt tail with a brown base, in the same place as the reference - unless this poncho has a tail of its own, which is then the ONLY tail and Pikachu's is hidden under the cape. Never two tails, never a mirrored copy. Two arms, two feet, two red cheeks, round black eyes and an open happy mouth, all exactly where the reference has them. It is the reference pose, not a front-facing symmetrical pose. Like the official Pokémon Center Poncho-wearing Pikachu, the poncho is a hooded cape: the HOOD IS THE POKÉMON'S HEAD, with its eyes and features on top of the hood, and Pikachu's face peeking out through a round opening at the front; Pikachu's ears are COMPLETELY HIDDEN inside the hood - no yellow ears or black ear tips poking out anywhere; the only ears, horns or crests on top are the costume Pokémon's own. The cape is short, over the shoulders and back. Keep every costume piece COMPACT and close to the body so the whole sprite stays about the size of the reference: wings small and folded, horns and tails short, nothing reaching past the edges of the frame. This one is a Mega Charizard X poncho, exactly like the official Pokémon Center figure: a charcoal-black hood shaped like Mega Charizard X's head, with a small red eye marking on each side of the top, two light-blue horn tips pointing back from the crown, two tiny white fangs at the hood's front edge, and a big puffy light-blue flame shape on each side of Pikachu's face like cheek flaps; a black cape with two small black wings, light-blue underneath, on the back; and Charizard X's black tail with a big light-blue flame tip hanging out behind the cape - this costume tail is the only tail, Pikachu's own is hidden; a blue-and-black swirled Mega Stone fastens it at the neck.
