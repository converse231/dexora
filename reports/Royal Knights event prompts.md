# The Royal Tournament: art prompts

The week after Grateful Harvest (Nov 29 - Dec 5): the castle throws open its gates for a tournament, and every Pokémon in the realm turns up in armour. High King Zacian is the hunt.

**The story (the poster's lore).** Once a year the old castle raises its banners and every Pokémon of the realm comes to compete. Pawns march onto a giant chessboard, a sword waits in its stone for whoever can draw it, the gate itself stands guard, and the queen watches from her balcony with her champion at her side. Win enough favours and the High King rides down from the clouds to see who is worthy.

**How to use**
- Start a new Gemini chat for every Pokémon, and attach its reference sprite.
- Save each result at the path beside its name. `npm run eventart` leaves the folder alone until the event is built.
- Save the title badge as `art/titles/event-knights.png`, and the background as `art/posters/knights.jpg`.

**The look.** The Pokémon keeps its own body, colours and pose (the game's sprite). It wears two or three bold royal pieces - silver armour, crimson tabards, plumes, crowns, ermine - plus ONE magical touch. Each line has its own order so they read apart at sprite size: the Chess Guard in black-and-cream checks, the Regalia in gold and ruby, the Castle Watch in crimson with lanterns, the Dragon Knights in silver, the Court in royal blue, the King's Line in purple. If Gemini redesigns the body, reply in the same chat: "keep the Pokémon's normal body and colours, only add the costume".

**Easter eggs.** Pawniard, Bisharp and Kingambit are a pawn, a bishop and a king; Honedge is the sword in the stone; Nidoking wears the King's Rock as its crown; Jigglypuff carries a quill for anyone who falls asleep during its song; Persian is sitting on a little Pay Day.

**30 Pokémon.** Six lines that evolve in costume (19 sprites), ten one of a kind (Black Knight Absol the rare one), and Zacian as the hunted legendary.
