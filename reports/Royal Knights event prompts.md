# Royal Knights event: art prompts

A castle tournament: squires, knights, a king and queen, and the High King as the hunt. Proposed for Nov 29 - Dec 5, the week after Grateful Harvest. Not built yet: this is the art.

**How to use**
- Start a new Gemini chat for every Pokémon, and attach its reference sprite.
- Save each result at the path beside its name. `npm run eventart` leaves that folder alone until the event is built.
- Save the title badge as `art/titles/event-knights.png`, and the background as `art/posters/knights.jpg`.

**The look.** The Pokémon keeps its own body, colours and pose (the game's sprite). It wears two or three bold royal pieces that read at a glance - silver armour, crimson tabards, plumes, crowns, ermine - plus ONE magical touch. If Gemini redesigns the body, reply in the same chat: "keep the Pokémon's normal body and colours, only add the costume".

**30 Pokémon.** Six lines that evolve in costume (19 sprites), ten one of a kind, one of them rare, and Zacian as the hunted legendary.
