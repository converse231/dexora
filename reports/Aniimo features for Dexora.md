# Turn Dexora's empty towns into errands

Aniimo is Pawprint Studio's free-to-play, open-world creature-catching action RPG. It launched on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series on 16 September 2026 and on mobile a week later. Its pitch is Pokemon Legends: Arceus-style catching in a Genshin-style world, plus one idea critics agree is new: **Twining**, where you become your creature to travel and fight. Critics and players like its creatures, its world, its visible catch odds, and the promise that creatures are caught and never bought. They dislike its live-service clutter: many currencies, a "carnival" HUD, one-at-a-time reward splash screens, and a rare-form system that read as hidden gacha. For Dexora, the most useful part of Aniimo is not its open world. It is the way the hub city Astra works. Astra has no wild creatures. It stays worth visiting because every building does one small job on a clock: a daily-rotating vendor, a weekly free ticket, a weekly rest that pays, a gift counter, an egg incubator, and a place to meet people. That is the answer to Dexora's six empty towns. Give each town a reason to walk there on a daily or weekly rhythm, tied to systems Dexora already has: duplicates, research, completion per map, the walking party and friends. Do not copy Astra's luck machines or its currency sprawl. The best ideas, ranked by fun per effort, are these. First, show a real catch percentage on the ball rail. Second, make each town the "Branch" for the maps around it, so finishing a map's catches unlocks a lure you collect in town. Third, add a town request board that buys your spares. Fourth, add a Four Island Day Care that hatches eggs by steps. Fifth, add photo spots for the walking party.

## Aniimo sells capture quality, not species

Aniimo launched after closed betas in January–February and July 2026. It peaked at about **127,914 concurrent Steam players on day one** ([GuruGamer](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/aniimo-packs-steam-at-launch-then-splits-players-over-monetization-27311)), and SteamDB later recorded about **162,895** on 20 September ([SteamDB](https://steamdb.com/en/tools/steam-charts/4126040-aniimo)). The player is a researcher based in Astra, "a technologically advanced floating city in the sky," who goes out on expeditions to the wild continent of Idyll ([The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/aniimo-preview-what-is-aniimo/)). Executive producer Jovi Zhang promised that "gacha mechanics will not be employed for obtaining Aniimo", with **200+ creatures** all findable in the world ([FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/aniimo-dev-confirms-no-gacha-creatures/)). One reviewer counted only 95+ at launch, so that figure is uncertain ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/aniimo-review/)). The money comes from somewhere else. Players pay for Lumin Crystals, a battle-pass "Companion Handbook", monthly passes, cosmetics, storage and speed-ups. Premium Aniipods can guarantee a catch with higher quality ([Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2026/07/17/aniimo-closed-beta-early-preview-genshin-meets-pokemon-with-a-little-palworld/)).

### The catch loop rewards approach and shows its odds

Catching happens in real time. You aim an Aniipod and **the capture percentage appears as soon as you aim**. It rises if the target is asleep, facing away or low on HP. It also rises when the target is "Broken": attacks drain a stagger gauge, and an emptied gauge lets you Quick Catch. Missed pods are picked up automatically, and a "Tumbler" pod catches several nearby creatures at once ([games.gg](https://games.gg/aniimo/guides/aniimo-catching-how-to-boost-your-capture-rate/); [aniimotools.dev](https://aniimotools.dev/help/combat/)). Sleeping creatures at night are much easier to catch ([Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)). Alphas are giant field bosses. After you defeat one you get **one throw**, and a miss means fighting it again. A caught Alpha stays oversized but has ordinary stats ([aniimo.kr, unverified fan data](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)).

Rarity comes in layers. "Sparkling" is the shiny equivalent, at about **1 in 20,000** wild. It comes in ten common styles plus rarer Dazzling and Shadow styles, and fan guides disagree on the exact odds ([AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/aniimo-sparkling-styles-dazzling-and-shadow-odds-for-all-12-types/)). Ten "Sparkling Sigils" turn a creature you own into a Sparkling one. Sigils come from reporting Sparklings at an outpost or from catching 1,000 of one species ([aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)). On top of Sparkling sit about **220 weather and regional Forms**: same stats, new look or element. Above those are 25 Prismana forms that spawn only during "Prismana Flow" weather ([AniimoVerse](https://www.aniimoverse.com/guides/prismana)).

### Completion opens the rare levers

Aniimo's best structural idea links completion to rare hunting. Each region has a "Branch". Catching **40% of an area's species** earns a Bronze badge. Then you hand in 30 Lumin Amber at the Branch to unlock "Nurture", a daily charge (up to three banked) with a **3% chance to start Prismana Flow and a pity counter**. A Silver badge unlocks weather control for that area ([AniimoVerse](https://www.aniimoverse.com/guides/prismana)). Fast-travel crystals in the world list the local creatures, your collection status for each, and when they spawn ([Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)). The result is that a map's catch log is also the key to that map's rare odds.

### Progression, battles and home sit around the catch

Evolution conditions are set per species: a level, a stone, a time of day, a Sanctum clear, or **defeating that species' Alpha**. Critics likened this to Digimon World ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/aniimo-review/); [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/aniimo-pc/)). Hidden "Innate Potential" (Common to Perfect) works like IVs. An MBTI-style personality gives +2–6% in combat and gates a few evolutions ([aniimo.kr](https://aniimo.kr/en/systems/aniimo-traits-explained)). Combat is real time with four creatures, in either Command mode or Twine mode, built on elemental counters and the Break gauge ([game.fr](https://www.game.fr/en/2026/09/aniimo-gameplay-twining-capture-avis/)).

Co-op is hosted for up to four players, and only the host's progress saves. **Trading needs "Affinity"**, a friendship score that grows by at most 100 a day from visiting the friend's home, chatting, gesturing and playing together. Each Affinity rank widens what you can trade: Trusted, for example, unlocks Umbral and weather forms. Trades are capped at five a day, and a traded creature is locked for 30 days ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/aniimo-trading-how-to-trade-aniimo-with-friends/)). "Egg Heist" is a late-game PvPvE mode where three-player teams race for rare eggs ([allthings.how PvP](https://allthings.how/does-aniimo-have-pvp-egg-heist-and-pathfinder-challenges-explained/)).

"Homeland" opens at Lv 20 through a portal in an RV. Caught creatures work about 20 facilities, using 13 work abilities: Fire cooks, Grass plants, and so on. Matching element and personality each add 20% output, and **output stops when the food runs out** ([allthings.how Homeland](https://allthings.how/aniimo-homeland-how-to-unlock-and-level-up-your-farm/)).

The daily and weekly loop has three layers. A five-task daily checklist resets at 04:00. Weekly "Season Stamps" are **capped separately per activity**: Home Orders 600, Egg Heist 600, Catch Report 400, a weekly café rest 200 ([nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/aniimo-stamp-shop-location-weekly-stock-and-monday-refresh-time/)). Mini-events rotate on top: "Who's That Aniimo?" silhouettes; a daily target list where unowned species count double; a "Glamour Star" dress-up contest; and "Vein Abundance", which boosts one region each week. Egg hatching is halved every weekend ([allthings.how calendar](https://allthings.how/aniimo-event-calendar-every-launch-season-event-and-its-dates/)). A full photo mode is built into daily tasks ([allthings.how photo mode](https://allthings.how/aniimo-group-photo-mode-guide-how-to-pose-a-full-party/)).

## Players loved the creatures and hated the carnival

Reception split along one line. The fantasy won, and the scaffolding around it lost. OpenCritic's **Top Critic Average is 80 (83% recommend, 12 reviews)**, with scores from Marooners' Rock's 9 down to Siliconera's 5 ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21267/view)). On Steam the game opened at **49% positive** ([MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/aniimo-steam-launch-reviews-spieler-zahlen/)) and recovered to about **74% of 20,264 reviews** by late September ([Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/4126040/Aniimo)). The recovery came as players found the core loop could be played free. GamingTrend wrote that "you can enjoy the game and *never* have to engage with" the paid layer ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/aniimo-review/)).

The praise is consistent. Players liked the creature designs and the world, and they liked that creatures are never gacha. Twining "gives catching a purpose beyond filling a log" ([GuruGamer](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/aniimo-packs-steam-at-launch-then-splits-players-over-monetization-27311)). Previews praised hidden chests and creature abilities that open routes, such as burning vines or making updrafts to glide on ([Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/previews/2026/06/09/aniimo-hands-on-preview-an-idyllic-creature-catcher/); [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/aniimo-could-be-a-solid-pokemon-and-palworld-rival-if-your-pc-can-handle-it-preview/)).

The complaints are just as consistent, and they matter more to Dexora because they are about interface, not budget. CGMagazine counted **16 menu icons**. It found achievement claims buried in repeated "Item Get" splash screens, so players spent "more time in menus than they are actually playing the game" ([CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/aniimo-pc/)). Press Start called it "the busiest" HUD it had seen, causing "decision paralysis" ([Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/previews/2026/06/09/aniimo-hands-on-preview-an-idyllic-creature-catcher/)). GamingTrend called it "like walking into a carnival" ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/aniimo-review/)). Even beta testers complained of unexplained currencies ([ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/aniimo-could-be-a-solid-pokemon-and-palworld-rival-if-your-pc-can-handle-it-preview/)).

Two design choices drew particular fire. Prismana Flow, a weather roll with pity that gates rare forms, was called "hidden gacha" even though the creatures themselves are caught ([AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/aniimo-s-steam-rating-recovers-to-mostly-positive-amid-ai-controversy/)). Siliconera criticised the farm for sitting "in a pocket dimension" cut off from the world, unlike Palworld's bases ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-aniimo-is-a-constantly-distracting-experience/)). Launch-day reviews also punished a "slow, boring start", bugs and lost progress ([MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/aniimo-steam-launch-reviews-spieler-zahlen/)).

The sources have limits. Most of the hard numbers (odds, prices, pity) come from fan guide sites written a few weeks after launch, and they sometimes conflict. No first-hand Reddit or Discord threads were read, so nothing here shows which features *keep* players or which ones they request most. The ranking below is an inference from what critics praised and resented, not from retention data.

## Five Aniimo ideas earn their place in Dexora

Most of what Aniimo offers, Dexora already has. That narrows the list a lot. "Who's That Aniimo?" is Dexora's daily quiz. Vein Abundance is close to the mass outbreak. Aniimo's Alphas, research and login tracks have equivalents in Dexora's alpha layer, research levels and check-in. Twining's "catching has a purpose" is what the walking party's field abilities already do in 2D. Dexora's twenty tiers already exceed Sparkling's twelve styles. Several Aniimo ideas are therefore left out on purpose. Nested sub-styles inside a tier would add chase targets where Dexora already has plenty. Personality modifiers would be a second derived trait beside gender. Real-time co-op does not fit a solo-first, async game. The table ranks what remains. The town-specific ideas get their own section after it.

| Rank | Idea | Aniimo evidence | Dexora system it extends | Effort | Why it ranks here |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Print the catch **percentage** beside each ball, not only the multiplier | The % shows on aim and is part of the catch feel ([games.gg](https://games.gg/aniimo/guides/aniimo-catching-how-to-boost-your-capture-rate/)) | `catchChance` and `liveMult` on the ball rail (`NEVER_CERTAIN` and `NEVER_HOPELESS` already bound it) | Small | One readout. Every throw becomes an easy-to-read decision. |
| 2 | **Map completion badges that unlock a rare lever** (see the Branch idea below) | 40% area completion, then Bronze, then a daily Nurture charge with pity ([AniimoVerse](https://www.aniimoverse.com/guides/prismana)) | Dex WHERE TO LOOK, `encounterTable` residents, `rarityPower`, the lift caps that check.mjs enforces | Medium | Turns "fill the map" into the key to that map's rare odds. This is the strongest idea in the source. |
| 3 | **A one-time claim-all audit** before adding anything claimable | "16 icons" and repeated "Item Get" splashes ([CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/aniimo-pc/)) | Bingo's `claimBingo`, `claimAchievements`, `claimDex`, the HUD zones | Small | Prevents the most-cited flaw. Dexora's HUD-zones and one-`changed()` rules are already the right defence. |
| 4 | **"Unowned counts double"** on the daily quest | "Idyll Ecological Investigation", where unowned species are worth double ([allthings.how calendar](https://allthings.how/aniimo-event-calendar-every-launch-season-event-and-its-dates/)) | Daily quests | Small | Points late players at their gaps without a new system. |
| 5 | **An Alpha as an optional evolution objective** | Evolutions that need you to beat that species' Alpha were praised as Digimon-like ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/aniimo-review/); [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/aniimo-pc/)) | `rollAlpha` and research `TASKS` (append a task, "catch an alpha of the line", that discounts that line's evolution candy) | Medium | Gives alphas a second purpose. Keep it a discount, not a gate, so no evolution depends on a 1-in-`ALPHA_CHANCE` roll. |
| 6 | **Friend affinity**: a small, daily-capped counter fed by async acts that unlocks what you can ask a friend for | The Affinity ladder gates which forms can be traded ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/aniimo-trading-how-to-trade-aniimo-with-friends/)) | Friends on the trainer card, `want_uids`, `trade_limit()` | Large (SQL first) | It gives friendship somewhere to grow, but the server work is real, and it must never lock trades players already have. |

The ranking puts the catch percentage first because it costs almost nothing and changes every encounter. Aniimo's catch odds were the one interface feature critics singled out for praise. The completion badge ranks second. It costs more, but it does the most: a map you have mostly caught becomes the map where you hunt rares, which is the late-game motive Dexora's per-map tables lack. The affinity ladder ranks last because it touches `db/trading.sql`. Under Dexora's rules, a new limit must be added in SQL first and kept equal in `trade.js`.

## Six towns become the places where things get done

Astra shows that a hub with no wild creatures can still be useful. It works because it is a strip of single-purpose counters, several of which **refresh on a clock**. Weston's stock changes daily. A strength-tester NPC hands out one free ticket a week. A café rest pays up to 200 stamps a week. There is a gift shop for friends, a Hatchinator for eggs, and an open square where strangers duel and trade ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-astra-shops-vendors-and-what-they-sell-in-aniimo/); [nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/aniimo-stamp-shop-location-weekly-stock-and-monday-refresh-time/); [games.gg](https://games.gg/aniimo/guides/aniimo-multiplayer-guide/)). A visit there is a short ritual, not a destination. Siliconera's complaint about the pocket-dimension farm shows the reverse: a home cut off from the world feels pointless ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-aniimo-is-a-constantly-distracting-experience/)). Dexora's towns already sit on the world map between roads. They only need counters worth walking to.

Several Dexora rules shape the design. A town rolls no encounter, rod or rift, and is always open. The game makes no sound, and adding a clock-driven map effect needs the user's sign-off first. Any new art is drawn by the user from prompts. Nothing a browser decides can be ranked. With those rules in place, the ideas fall into three layers.

### Every town becomes a Branch for its neighbouring maps

This is Aniimo's completion-to-lever loop, placed in towns. Each town is assigned the maps its roads reach; the world doors in `ROADS` already define that neighbourhood. A map earns a Bronze mark when you have caught a share of its residents. The share is read from `encounterTable` and the dex, and stays derived, never stored, like achievements. You visit the map's town to switch the map's **lure** on. The lure is one daily charge, banked up to three like Nurture. It lifts that map's tier roll for a set number of steps, goes through `rarityPower`, and stays under `DEX_CHARM` like every other lift that check.mjs bounds. It runs as a field ring like a honey, so it reuses `fx-ring` and the `state.field` family slot instead of adding a new kind of UI.

The town visit is the point. You walk to Fortree to switch on Monsoon Trail's lure, then walk back out to hunt. Aniimo's fast-travel crystals suggest the board in each town should show what the Dex's WHERE TO LOOK already knows: the neighbouring maps' residents, which ones you have caught, and each map's badge progress ([Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1777500)). This also answers the user's complaint directly. All six towns get the same core job, and each feeds a different set of maps.

Two cautions apply. First, Aniimo's version was called hidden gacha because a 3% roll gated whole forms. Dexora's lure should lift odds that already exist, never open a creature that is otherwise unreachable. Second, Dexora's rule that income must be renewable means the lure's extra variant bounty needs measuring against `variantPay` before its strength is set.

### One signature counter per town, where the real building already stands

Each town's second job should come from something only it offers, and Gen 3 maps already suggest one. **Four Island** held FireRed's Day Care, so it suits egg hatching. Leave two spares of one line and get an egg that hatches after a number of steps, rolling its own tier like a catch. Aniimo's ten-minute habitat eggs and weekend hatch boost show eggs work as a reason to come back ([allthings.how calendar](https://allthings.how/aniimo-event-calendar-every-launch-season-event-and-its-dates/)). Steps fit Dexora's rule that the world clock runs on steps, and spares fit its duplicate-heavy Box. This is the largest piece here. Hatching is a new way for a creature to enter the save, so it needs a `savedField` line, research credit, and a check that it does not out-earn catching.

**Lilycove** has Emerald's department store and contest hall. The department store becomes a daily-rotating shelf: a few discounted items and one rotating stone, picked by a `dayKey` hash like the quest. That copies Weston, Astra's daily vendor ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-astra-shops-vendors-and-what-they-sell-in-aniimo/)). The contest hall becomes a weekly themed contest after Aniimo's Glamour Star ([allthings.how calendar](https://allthings.how/aniimo-event-calendar-every-launch-season-event-and-its-dates/)). Your walking party is scored against the week's theme (a type, a size, a tier) with fixed thresholds that pay ribbons. There is no leaderboard, because Dexora's trust rules forbid ranking anything the browser decides.

**Mauville**, Dexora's world hub, gets a **request board**: a few orders a day for a spare of a named species or type, paid in money and candy. This is Aniimo's Home Orders, tied for its largest weekly stamp source ([nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/aniimo-stamp-shop-location-weekly-stock-and-monday-refresh-time/)), turned into a third use for duplicates beside sell and convert. Orders go through `keeper()` and `variantSpareUids`, so they never ask for a variant, an alpha or the walking party.

**Fortree**, **Fallarbor** and **Lavender** can stay Branch-only at first. Lavender is the natural candidate if a later idea needs a quiet, memorial-style building.

### Visit rituals that cost almost nothing

The cheapest layer copies Aniimo's photo verb and weekly café rest. Each town gets a **photo spot**: a tile where you pose your walking party, which stamps a six-town card. Aniimo builds photo-taking into its daily checklist ([allthings.how photo mode](https://allthings.how/aniimo-group-photo-mode-guide-how-to-pose-a-full-party/)). In Dexora the photo can be the existing follower drawn on that tile. The stamp card fits naturally as a Bingo goal through `noteBingo`, which is cheaper than a new claim screen. A once-a-week "rest" counter in one town, paying a small fixed sum, copies the Star-Crossed Tea Party. The counters can be door rooms, which `attach_inside` already supports, so no NPC sprite is required on day one.

Together these layers give Dexora's towns what Astra has: daily and weekly reasons to stop by, each tied to the collection. Dexora's version has no shop prices and no luck machines.

## Aniimo's mistakes Dexora must not repeat

Several things Aniimo was punished for are easy to repeat in a non-commercial game. **Do not add currencies.** Aniimo's Credits, Voxel Coins, Glimmer, Home Coins, Season Stamps and crystals were confusing even in beta ([ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/aniimo-could-be-a-solid-pokemon-and-palworld-rival-if-your-pc-can-handle-it-preview/)). Every town idea above pays in money, candy, dust or ribbons Dexora already has.

**Do not put luck machines in towns.** Astra's Starwish Gacha and Super Lucky Star charge 100 coins a pull ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-astra-shops-vendors-and-what-they-sell-in-aniimo/)). Dexora already has the weekly roulette, and Prismana Flow showed that anything shaped like a slot machine near creatures reads as gacha ([AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/aniimo-s-steam-rating-recovers-to-mostly-positive-amid-ai-controversy/)).

**Do not add HUD chips or claim splashes one at a time** ([CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/aniimo-pc/)). A town's alert joins an existing HUD zone or nothing.

**Do not copy Aniimo's size items** (Growth Agaric, Shrinkstool: [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-astra-shops-vendors-and-what-they-sell-in-aniimo/)). In Dexora, gender is derived from size, so a size changer would also change a Pokemon's sex.

**Do not gate content behind energy or upkeep that punishes absence.** Energy gates were a top launch complaint ([GuruGamer](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/aniimo-packs-steam-at-launch-then-splits-players-over-monetization-27311)), and Homeland's starving facilities are a chore by design. Banked lure charges and step-based eggs reward coming back without punishing a week away.

**Do not build a disconnected home** ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-aniimo-is-a-constantly-distracting-experience/)). Anything home-like belongs in a town on the map.

**Finally, a slow start and lost progress cost Aniimo more than any missing feature** ([MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/aniimo-steam-launch-reviews-spieler-zahlen/)). Dexora's save rules are already its best defence, and every new saved field needs its `savedField` line.

## Conclusion

The surprise is that Aniimo's most portable lesson comes from its least-praised space. Critics barely mention Astra, yet Astra solves Dexora's exact problem. A creature-free hub earns repeat visits when its counters refresh on a clock and feed the collection. Aniimo hurt itself by putting those counters behind currencies and gambles. Dexora has neither, so it can take the rhythm without the resentment.

The deeper idea is Aniimo's link from completion to rare odds. It changes what "finishing a map" means. Today a finished map in Dexora is somewhere you stop going. With a town Branch, it becomes the best place to hunt, and the town becomes the place you walk to first. That gives the towns a purpose and gives the late game a direction in one system.
