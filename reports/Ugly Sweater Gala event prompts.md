# The Ugly Sweater Gala: art prompts

The week after the Royal Tournament (Dec 6 - 12): a black-tie winter gala where the dress code is the ugliest Christmas sweater you own. Wishing Star Jirachi is the hunt.

**The story (the poster's lore).** Every winter the old mansion on the hill holds the most elegant party of the year, and every winter the invitation says the same thing: black tie, and the worst sweater you can find. The tree is lit, the carols start, the dessert table is already being eaten, and someone in a glitched sweater keeps flickering. At midnight the star on top of the tree is said to wake up and grant one wish to the best dressed.

**How to use**
- Start a new Gemini chat for every Pokémon, and attach its reference sprite.
- Save each result at the path beside its name. `npm run eventart` leaves the folder alone until the event is built.
- Save the title badge as `art/titles/event-sweaters.png`, and the background as `art/posters/sweaters.jpg`.

**The look.** The Pokémon keeps its own body, colours and pose (the game's sprite). It wears one bold knitted sweater (or a cosy, scarf or hat where a sweater cannot go) plus ONE magical touch. Every sweater hides an easter egg - something from the Pokémon's own story knitted into it - and the knitting stays big and chunky so it reads at sprite size. If Gemini redesigns the body, reply in the same chat: "keep the Pokémon's normal body and colours, only add the costume".

**Easter eggs.** Cubchoo and Beartic wear each other's faces; Snover's baubles are Oran Berries; Swinub's sweater is Tiny Mushrooms and Piloswine's a Big Mushroom; Mamoswine's is the cave painting it walked out of; Vanilluxe shares one two-necked sweater; one of Litwick's candles burns blue; Sealeo balances its pom-pom; Delibird's Present might heal you or explode; Stantler's sleeves are hypnosis spirals; Lapras carries a ferry ticket; Porygon's sweater glitched; Slurpuff wears its Whipped Dream; Snom took a bite of its snowflake; Chansey's egg is the Lucky Egg; Smeargle painted everyone else's sweater; Ditto's sweater has a certain pink cat on it, and nobody can say why; Jirachi's moon is the thousand years it sleeps.

**27 Pokémon.** Six lines that evolve in costume (16 sprites), ten one of a kind (Mystery Gift Ditto the rare one), and Jirachi as the hunted legendary.
